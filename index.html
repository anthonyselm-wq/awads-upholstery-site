Six Decades on the Same Corner

Awad's Upholstery was founded in 1960 by Edmund L. Awad on the east side of Cincinnati, at the intersection of St. Rt. 125 and St. Rt. 132. Through six decades of changing cars, boats, and businesses, we've kept Amelia and the greater Cincinnati area covered.

We're now located at 3413 St. Rt. 132 — right across the street from our original spot — so you'll still find us exactly where you expect us. Whether it's spring boat season, winter car and truck time, or a restaurant refreshing its booths, our shop is set up to handle it.
