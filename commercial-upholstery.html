Commercial Upholstery for Businesses That Can't Afford Downtime

Commercial seating lives a different life than a car seat or a boat cushion — it gets sat in dozens or hundreds of times a day, every day, by people who have never met the business owner and have no reason to treat the furniture gently. Restaurant booths, office chairs, and waiting room seating wear out fast under that kind of constant use, and when they do, it reflects directly on how customers and clients perceive the business. Awad's Upholstery has done commercial work alongside our marine, automotive, and top upholstery since 1960, and we understand that a commercial job has different priorities than a personal vehicle or boat: durability, cleanability, and getting the business back up and running with minimal disruption.

We handle restaurant booth and chair reupholstery, bar stools, office and waiting room seating, salon and barbershop chairs, church pew cushions, and other commercial seating that takes heavy daily traffic. Every job is fabricated in our shop using materials chosen for how they'll actually be used, not just how they look on day one.

Signs Your Commercial Seating Needs Attention

Business owners usually notice these issues before a piece of furniture becomes a real problem:

  • Cracked or torn vinyl on booth seats and backs, especially along the edges where customers slide in and out
  • Foam that's flattened from constant use, leaving seats uncomfortable or visibly sunken
  • Stained or discolored upholstery that no longer cleans up, even with commercial cleaners
  • Seams splitting from repeated stress in high-traffic areas
  • Mismatched patches or repairs that stand out against the rest of the seating
  • Seating that no longer matches a refreshed interior, sign, or brand color scheme
  • Office or waiting room chairs that look worn enough to make a poor first impression on clients

Our Commercial Upholstery Process

  1. Free estimate and consultation. We'll look at your seating — whether it's a single reception chair or a full dining room of booths — and talk through timeline, material, and budget.
  2. Material selection for durability. We help choose commercial-grade vinyl rated for the level of daily use your business sees, in a color that fits your space.
  3. Foam and support replacement. Flattened or broken-down foam is replaced with foam suited to commercial seating, balancing comfort with how it holds up under constant use.
  4. Fabrication in-house. Panels are cut and sewn to match your existing seating exactly, so multiple units come out consistent.
  5. Scheduled installation. For multi-unit jobs like a full restaurant, we work with you on scheduling to minimize the impact on business hours.

Materials Built for Commercial Traffic

Commercial-Grade Vinyl

Commercial vinyl is built heavier and more abrasion-resistant than residential or automotive vinyl, designed to handle constant contact, spills, and cleaning chemicals without breaking down. It's the standard choice for restaurant booths, bar stools, and any seating that gets used dozens of times a day.

Easy-Clean, Wipeable Surfaces

For restaurants, medical offices, and other businesses where spills and sanitizing are a daily reality, we work with vinyl that wipes clean without staining or absorbing odors — a meaningful difference from fabric options that hold onto spills and smells over time.

Foam Density for High-Traffic Seating

Commercial foam is selected for a different balance than residential furniture — it needs to stay comfortable through constant use without breaking down as fast as softer foam would. We match foam density to how the seating is actually used, not just how it feels on the showroom floor.

Restaurant Booth & Bar Stool Reupholstery

Restaurant seating is one of our most common commercial jobs — booths and bar stools take more daily wear than almost any other type of commercial furniture, between constant sitting, spills, and cleaning. We can reupholster booths and stools to match your existing color scheme exactly, or help you refresh the look as part of a broader interior update. Multi-unit jobs, like reupholstering an entire dining room of booths, are something we handle regularly, and we'll work with you on a schedule that keeps the disruption to normal business hours as low as possible.

Office, Waiting Room & Specialty Seating

Beyond restaurants, we handle office chairs, waiting room and lobby seating, salon and barbershop chairs, and church pew cushions. These jobs are often about presentation as much as durability — worn reception seating is one of the first things a client or patient notices walking into a business, and refreshing it is a relatively low-cost way to improve that first impression without a full renovation.

Planning a Renovation or Rebrand?

If you're updating your business's colors, signage, or overall look, reupholstering existing seating is often far more cost-effective than replacing furniture outright — the frames and structure underneath a booth or chair usually have plenty of life left even when the fabric or vinyl doesn't. We can work from paint swatches, new signage colors, or a designer's mood board to make sure updated seating fits the rest of the renovation instead of looking like an afterthought.

Caring for Commercial Seating Between Reupholstery Jobs

Regular cleaning with products rated for commercial vinyl — rather than harsh chemicals that can dry out and crack the surface over time — keeps seating looking better longer and pushes back the timeline before a full reupholstery is needed. Addressing small tears or loose seams quickly, before they're stressed further by daily use, also tends to be far less expensive than waiting until an entire booth or chair needs to be redone. For businesses with multiple units, rotating which pieces get the heaviest use when possible can help even out wear across a dining room or waiting area.

Common Questions

Commercial Upholstery FAQ

Can you handle a full restaurant's worth of booths?

Yes, multi-unit jobs like a full dining room of booths are common commercial work for us. We'll talk through scheduling upfront so the job fits around your business hours as much as possible.

How do you minimize downtime for a busy restaurant or office?

For larger jobs, we can often work in batches — reupholstering a portion of your seating at a time — or schedule pickup and delivery around your slower hours or closed days, depending on the scope of the project.

Can you match our existing color and material?

In most cases, yes. Bring in a sample of your current material or photos, and we'll work to match the color and texture as closely as possible, or help you choose a coordinated update.

Is there a minimum order for commercial work?

No — we handle everything from a single office chair to a full restaurant, so there's no job too small to bring in for an estimate.

Do you do on-site measuring for larger commercial jobs?

For larger or multi-unit jobs, we can talk through the best way to measure and plan the project, whether that means bringing in a sample piece or discussing the space directly. Call ahead and we'll figure out the right approach for your job.

What vinyl do you recommend for high-traffic seating?

We recommend commercial-grade vinyl rated for heavy daily use and easy cleaning, and we'll help you pick a specific option based on how the seating is used — a fine-dining booth and a fast-casual bar stool don't always need the same material.

Can you reupholster seating to match a new paint color or brand refresh?

Yes. Bring in paint swatches, signage colors, or design references and we'll help select vinyl that fits the rest of your renovation rather than clashing with it.

Is it cheaper to reupholster than to buy new furniture?

Often, yes — especially for booths and chairs where the underlying frame is still structurally sound. Reupholstering lets you keep furniture that fits your space correctly while updating the look and durability for a fraction of full replacement cost.
